Enter the total for all advertising channels on this date. Each save replaces the day's total in reports and preserves its history. Enter 0 for a day with no spend. Choose Today in the period filter to start.

Read the numbers correctly

Cost per verified sale · blended
Total ad spend ÷ all payments verified in the period. Includes organic sales; this is not new-customer CAC.
Tagged period ROAS
Revenue from orders with paid campaign tags ÷ total ad spend. First-touch website attribution; not Meta's attribution or a controlled measure of ad impact.
Blended revenue / ad spend
All verified revenue ÷ total ad spend. Organic sales are included.
Missing spend
Ratios stay unavailable until every day in the selected period has a total. Today is still accumulating.

Campaign & creative sales

Verification-period revenue by original source, campaign and approved utm_content tag. Creative-level ad spend is not imported, so no creative ROAS is claimed.
